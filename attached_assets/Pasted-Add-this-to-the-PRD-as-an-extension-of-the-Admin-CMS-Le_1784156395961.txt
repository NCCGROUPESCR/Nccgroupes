Add this to the PRD as an extension of the Admin CMS.


---

Legal & Compliance CMS

Do NOT hardcode legal pages into the website.

All legal, compliance, and policy pages must be fully manageable from the Admin Dashboard CMS.

The system should allow authorized Admin users to create, edit, publish, unpublish, archive, and preview all legal documents without modifying source code.

Policy Management

The CMS must include dedicated management pages for:

Terms & Conditions

Privacy Policy

Cookie Policy

AML (Anti-Money Laundering) Policy

KYC (Know Your Customer) Policy

CTF (Counter-Terrorist Financing) Policy

Sanctions Compliance Policy

Buyer Protection Policy

Seller Protection Policy

Escrow Service Agreement

Escrow Release Policy

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Complaint & Dispute Resolution Policy

Data Protection Policy

Data Retention Policy

Information Security Policy

Fraud Prevention Policy

Risk Management Policy

Disclaimer

Acceptable Use Policy



---

CMS Features

Each policy page should support:

Page Title

URL Slug

Rich Text Editor (HTML support)

Featured Image (optional)

SEO Title

SEO Description

SEO Keywords

Tags

Author

Version Number

Status (Draft, Published, Archived)

Publish Date

Last Updated Date

Revision History

Preview Before Publishing



---

Version Control

The CMS should maintain a complete version history for every legal document.

Admin users should be able to:

View previous versions.

Compare revisions.

Restore an earlier version.

View the editor and timestamp for each revision.



---

Publishing

Admin should be able to:

Save as Draft.

Publish immediately.

Unpublish.

Archive.

Schedule publication (optional).


Published changes should appear on the website immediately.


---

Footer Integration

All published legal pages should automatically appear in the website footer.

Example:

Legal

Information Security Policy

Fraud Prevention Policy

Risk Management Policy

Disclaimer

Acceptable Use Policy


The footer should update automatically whenever a policy is published or unpublished.


---

Permissions

Only Admin users may:

Create legal pages.

Edit legal pages.

Publish or unpublish policies.

Delete or archive policies.

Restore previous versions.


Support, Buyers, Sellers, and Logistics users have read-only access to published policies.


---

Acceptance Criteria

✅ All legal and compliance documents are managed from the Admin CMS.

✅ No legal page content is hardcoded.

✅ Every policy supports SEO metadata and custom slugs.

✅ Version history and revision restoration are available.

✅ Published policies automatically appear in the website footer.

✅ Admin can manage all legal documents without requiring code changes.
