Feature Specification – Escrow Funding Confirmation & Proof of Payment

Overview

When the buyer funds the escrow account provided by Support, the platform must guide the buyer through confirming payment and sharing proof with all relevant parties.

---

Workflow

1. Escrow Account Display

After Support sets the escrow account for the transaction:

- Display the escrow account details to the buyer.
- Show:
  - Bank Name
  - Account Name
  - Account Number
  - Amount to Pay
  - Reference (if applicable)

Display a prominent "I've Funded the Escrow" button.

---

2. Funding Confirmation Popup

When the buyer clicks "I've Funded the Escrow", open a confirmation modal.

Title:
Confirm Escrow Funding

Message:

«Have you completed the transfer to the escrow account? Please upload your payment proof before continuing.»

Buttons:

- Upload Proof
- Cancel

The buyer cannot continue without uploading proof of payment.

---

3. Proof of Payment Upload

Allow the buyer to upload:

- Screenshot
- Bank receipt
- PDF receipt
- Image (JPG, PNG, WEBP)
- Other supported payment evidence

After upload:

- Store the file securely.
- Link it to the transaction.
- Record:
  - Upload time
  - Uploaded by
  - Transaction ID

---

4. Transaction Chat

Once uploaded, automatically post a system message in the transaction chat:

«Buyer has uploaded proof of escrow funding.»

Display the uploaded proof directly in the chat with a preview and download option.

---

5. Visibility

The uploaded proof must be immediately visible to:

- Buyer
- Seller
- Support
- Logistics

All participants should be able to:

- View the proof
- Download the proof
- Open it in full size

The buyer cannot delete or replace the proof after submission unless Support authorizes it.

---

6. Notifications

Immediately notify:

- Seller
- Support
- Logistics

Notification example:

«Buyer has funded the escrow account and uploaded proof of payment.»

Clicking the notification opens the transaction chat.

---

7. Transaction Status Update

After successful upload:

Transaction status changes from:

Awaiting Escrow Funding

to

Escrow Funding Submitted

Support can then verify the payment and either approve or reject it.

---

Acceptance Criteria

- ✅ Buyer sees the "I've Funded the Escrow" button after escrow details are provided.
- ✅ Clicking the button opens the funding confirmation popup.
- ✅ Buyer must upload proof before submission.
- ✅ Proof is securely stored and linked to the transaction.
- ✅ Proof appears instantly in the transaction chat.
- ✅ Buyer, Seller, Support, and Logistics can all view and download the proof.
- ✅ Seller, Support, and Logistics receive instant notifications.
- ✅ Transaction status updates to Escrow Funding Submitted after successful submission.
