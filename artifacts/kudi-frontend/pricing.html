Common questions

Everything you need to know

When am I charged?

The fee is deducted from the trading amount when funds are released to the seller. You never pay upfront — only when the deal closes successfully.

Who pays the fee — buyer or seller?

By default the fee is split equally. Either party can agree to cover the full fee; this is set when creating the transaction.

Is there a minimum transaction size?

Create a trading transaction using the amount and release conditions agreed by both parties.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect on your next transaction.

What counts as a "transaction"?

Any trading agreement created on the platform, whether buying goods or paying for services.

Do you offer refunds?

If a transaction fails before funds are released, the full amount (minus any processing costs already incurred) is returned to the buyer.
