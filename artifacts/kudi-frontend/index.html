The trading flow

Three steps stand between "agreed" and "paid."

01 — Fund

Buyer funds transaction

Archanda AG manages payments securely until the agreed transaction conditions are confirmed. See the payment terms before confirming.

02 — Agree & talk

Buyer and seller agree terms

A private deal chat keeps transaction details, documents, and discussion in one place.

03 — Release

Funds release on confirmation

The buyer confirms the agreed conditions before funds are released. Either party can open a dispute when needed.
